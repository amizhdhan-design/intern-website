How It Works

Eliminating Placement Friction for Relocating Students

Engineering and technical curricula across Tamil Nadu mandate short-term in-plant training and seasonal internships. When relocating to unfamiliar industrial hubs like Chennai, Coimbatore, or Madurai, students face challenges finding legitimate companies and safe routes.

Persistent Profile Onboarding

Students submit their details once (college, year, department, and contact information), establishing a permanent session profile on their browser.

District Proximity Search

Select any of the 38 districts in Tamil Nadu to immediately filter verified industrial units and tech parks.

Instant Allocation Requests

Submit direct training applications to recruiters with pre-populated student profile credentials.
